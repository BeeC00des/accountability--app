# 🔥 doerstab

**Imagine your future, get inspired and act — with friends who keep you going.**

doerstab helps people turn the life they picture into small daily steps, and stay consistent with a little help from their friends.

## What you can do

- **Vision board:** collect the images and words that capture your year, and turn them into a wallpaper.
- **Goals and check-ins:** break big goals into small steps and check in each day or week.
- **Streaks and reports:** watch your consistency grow over the week, month or year.
- **Friends:** cheer each other on and take on friendly challenges.

## Status

doerstab is a private project in active development. Access is by invitation while we test.

## Getting started (team members only)

1. Ask the project owner for access and the setup guide.
2. Never commit environment settings or keys.
